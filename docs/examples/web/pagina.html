HISTORY

Neon Circuitry emerged from the underground neon-lit clubs of 2021, bridging the gap between hardware synthesis and heavy rock instrumentation. Founded by multi-instrumentalists Jax and Thorne, the project quickly gained a cult following for their sensory-overload live performances.

"We wanted to build something that felt like a localized power surge—something both beautiful and dangerous."
